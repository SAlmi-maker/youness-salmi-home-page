About Me

Transforming Ideas intoSolutions

As a Mechanical Engineering Specialized Technician trained at the Cité des Métiers et des Compétences de L'Oriental, I have developed a strong foundation in mechanical design, manufacturing processes, and product development. My experience includes designing innovative projects in SolidWorks, exploring mechanical system optimization, and solving real-world engineering challenges with a focus on functionality, efficiency, and manufacturability.

Design & CAD

I use CAD tools to transform engineering concepts into detailed and functional 3D models. Through SolidWorks and technical drawing creation, I develop designs that balance performance, manufacturability, and practical implementation. My work focuses on creating efficient solutions while respecting real-world engineering constraints.

Product Development

From initial concept to final design, I enjoy solving mechanical challenges through innovation and structured engineering methods. Projects such as VoltBox and a hybrid turbo-compressor concept have strengthened my ability to analyze requirements, generate solutions, and refine designs through iterative development.

Manufacturing & Production

My training in mechanical manufacturing has provided a solid understanding of production methods, machining processes, materials, and industrial workflows. I design with manufacturing in mind, ensuring that engineering solutions remain practical, cost-effective, and ready for production environments.

Engineering Problem Solving

Engineering is more than design — it's about finding effective solutions to technical challenges. Whether optimizing mechanical systems, addressing integration constraints, or improving product functionality, I apply analytical thinking and creativity to develop reliable and efficient outcomes.
